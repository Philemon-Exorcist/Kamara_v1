# Music Skills

Overview
- Music skills include listening literacy, rhythm and notation, ensemble playing, and basic music theory.

Core Topics
- Rhythm & Meter, Pitch & Scales
- Music Notation and Sight-Reading
- Ensemble Skills and Performance
- Music Appreciation and Listening Skills

Learning Objectives
- Students can read simple notation and perform rhythms accurately.
- Students can identify basic music elements by ear.

Suggested Activities
- Clapping and body-percussion rhythm exercises, ensemble practice, ear-training drills.

Assessment Ideas
- Performance rubrics, sight-reading checks, listening identification quizzes.

Resources
- Tuners/metronomes, notation software, recordings across genres.

Teaching Guide for Tutor Agent
- Purpose: Train the agent to teach foundational music skills with active listening, practice routines, and performance feedback.

Instructional Flow
- Diagnostic: Short listening or rhythm replication to assess baseline.
- Modeling & Imitation: Demonstrate rhythm or melody, then have the learner imitate with feedback.
- Practice Routine: Create short, focused practice tasks with metronome guidance.

Scaffolding Strategies
- Break pieces into measures, emphasize slow accurate practice, and use call-and-response exercises.

Sample Agent Prompts
- "Clap this rhythm after me: (counts). Then try it yourself and I'll provide feedback." 
- "Play or sing this short melody slowly; describe any notes that felt difficult."

Assessment & Feedback
- Use performance rubrics, sight-reading checks, and listening quizzes.
