# English Skills

Overview
- English skills focus on reading, writing, speaking and listening, plus vocabulary and grammar mastery. These skills support communication across all subjects and are essential for academic success.

Core Skill Areas
- Reading Comprehension: identify main ideas, infer meaning, summarize, analyze structure and purpose.
- Vocabulary & Language: explicit word learning, use of context clues, morphological analysis.
- Grammar & Conventions: sentence structure, punctuation, tense, agreement, clarity.
- Writing: planning, drafting, revising, producing coherent paragraphs and essays for different purposes (narrative, expository, argumentative).
- Speaking & Listening: presenting, participating in discussions, active listening, asking clarifying questions.

Learning Objectives (examples)
- Students can summarize a text and identify the author’s main argument.
- Students can write a clear, structured paragraph with topic sentence and supporting details.
- Students can participate in a group discussion using academic language.

Suggested Activities
- Close reading exercises with guided questions.
- Writing workshops with peer review and revision cycles.
- Vocabulary journals and weekly mini-quizzes.
- Debate and presentation tasks to practice speaking skills.

Assessment Ideas
- Annotated reading responses, timed writing prompts, oral presentations, and vocabulary checks.

Resources
- Simple English readers, online corpora, grammar references, dictionaries, writing rubrics.

Notes
- Adapt reading level and genre to student age and proficiency. Emphasize cross-curricular texts to build domain-specific literacy.

Teaching Guide for Tutor Agent
- Purpose: Guide the tutor agent on how to teach English skills effectively to learners of varying levels.

Instructional Flow
- Diagnostic: Ask the learner a short reading or writing sample to gauge level.
- I Do / We Do / You Do: Model a small task (e.g., paragraph structure), co-create with the learner, then request independent practice.
- Feedback Loop: Provide specific, actionable feedback (highlight errors, explain why, suggest revision).

Scaffolding Strategies
- Break tasks into micro-steps (topic sentence, supporting detail, conclusion).
- Use sentence starters and templates for lower-level writers.
- Offer graded reading passages and targeted vocabulary lists.

Personalization & Signals
- Use `current_phase` or explicit learner metadata to adapt complexity.
- Monitor indicators: response length, error types, time-to-respond, and engagement to adjust support.

Sample Agent Prompts
- "Please write one paragraph (5–7 sentences) about your favorite hobby. I'll give feedback on structure and grammar." 
- "Read this short passage and tell me the main idea in one sentence. What words were unfamiliar?"

Formative Assessment
- Frequent low-stakes checks: short summaries, quick quizzes, and revision tasks.

Remediation & Extension
- Remediation: provide additional sentence-level practice, focused grammar explanations, and simplified texts.
- Extension: offer research prompts, essay challenges, or creative writing with peer-review suggestions.

Safety and Tone
- Keep feedback positive and growth-oriented. Avoid jargon and be explicit about steps for improvement.
