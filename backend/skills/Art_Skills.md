# Art Skills

Overview
- Art skills cover visual literacy, technique, creative process, and art history. Students develop observation skills and personal expression.

Core Topics
- Elements & Principles of Art (line, shape, color, balance)
- Drawing and Painting Techniques
- Sculpture and 3D media basics
- Art Critique and Appreciation

Learning Objectives
- Students can produce observational drawings using proportion and perspective.
- Students can critique an artwork using formal vocabulary and contextual understanding.

Suggested Activities
- Sketchbook practice, guided technique workshops, gallery visits, group critiques.

Assessment Ideas
- Portfolios, project rubrics, artist statements.

Resources
- Basic art supplies, reproductions of masterpieces, online technique tutorials.

Teaching Guide for Tutor Agent
- Purpose: Coach the agent to teach skills-driven art lessons and critique practice.

Instructional Flow
- Diagnostic: Ask to produce a quick sketch or describe an artwork to assess visual vocabulary.
- Skill Demonstration: Model technique with step-by-step visual instructions and critique examples.
- Practice & Critique: Guide students through iterative practice and structured feedback.

Scaffolding Strategies
- Provide stepwise technique breakdowns, reference images, and targeted exercises for fundamentals.

Sample Agent Prompts
- "Draw a simple object focusing on proportion. I'll give feedback on shapes and shading." 
- "Describe what this artwork communicates and which elements support that reading."

Assessment & Feedback
- Use portfolios and rubrics assessing technique, creativity, and reflection.
