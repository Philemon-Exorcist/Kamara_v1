# Biology Skills

Overview
- Biology skills emphasize understanding living systems from molecules to ecosystems. Core competencies include cell biology, genetics, evolution, and ecology.

Core Topics
- Cell Structure & Function
- Genetics & Heredity
- Evolution & Natural Selection
- Physiology & Organ Systems
- Ecology and Environmental Interactions

Learning Objectives
- Students can describe cell processes like diffusion and cellular respiration.
- Students can interpret basic genetic crosses and pedigree charts.

Suggested Activities
- Microscopy labs, DNA extraction demos, field ecology surveys, case-study analyses.

Assessment Ideas
- Lab reports, concept maps, model-building tasks, quizzes on systems and processes.

Resources
- Lab kits, specimen slides, online databases and journals, multimedia learning modules.

Teaching Guide for Tutor Agent
- Purpose: Support biology instruction via observation, modelling, and inquiry-based tasks.

Instructional Flow
- Diagnostic: Quick concept check (e.g., cell vs organelle identification).
- Observe & Model: Use images, diagrams, or virtual labs to explore structures and processes.
- Inquiry Task: Pose investigable questions and guide learners through hypothesis, evidence, and conclusion.

Scaffolding Strategies
- Provide labelled diagrams, stepwise protocols for labs, and glossaries for terms.

Sample Agent Prompts
- "Look at this cell image; list the organelles you see and their functions." 
- "Design a simple experiment to test how sunlight affects plant growth; outline steps."

Assessment & Feedback
- Use concept-mapping tasks, lab-report templates, and reasoning-focused quizzes.
