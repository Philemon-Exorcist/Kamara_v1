# Geography Skills

Overview
- Geography skills combine spatial thinking, map literacy, and understanding of human-environment interactions.

Core Topics
- Physical Geography: landforms, climate systems, biomes
- Human Geography: population, urbanization, cultural landscapes
- Map Skills & GIS basics
- Environmental and Sustainability Concepts

Learning Objectives
- Students can read and interpret maps, charts, and spatial data.
- Students can explain interactions between human activity and environmental systems.

Suggested Activities
- Map interpretation tasks, small GIS projects with open tools, case studies on environmental change.

Assessment Ideas
- Map quizzes, data interpretation tasks, short projects analyzing local geography.

Resources
- OpenStreetMap, QGIS tutorials, atlas and satellite imagery resources.

Teaching Guide for Tutor Agent
- Purpose: Help the agent teach spatial thinking, map literacy, and human-environment analysis.

Instructional Flow
- Diagnostic: Ask learners to interpret a simple map or chart to assess baseline map skills.
- Guided Analysis: Walk through map features, scales, and symbols; model spatial reasoning aloud.
- Application Task: Assign a local case study where students analyze human-environment interactions.

Scaffolding Strategies
- Provide step-by-step map reading prompts and sample analyses for comparison.

Sample Agent Prompts
- "Describe the main geographic features on this map and explain how they influence local activities." 
- "Use this dataset to create a simple chart of population change; what patterns do you see?"

Assessment & Feedback
- Map interpretation tasks, short GIS exercises, and written explanations of spatial patterns.
