# Mathematics Skills

Overview
- Mathematics skills develop number sense, logical reasoning, problem-solving, and quantitative literacy. These foundations support STEM learning and data-driven decision making.

Core Topics
- Number Sense & Arithmetic: place value, fractions, decimals, ratios, mental math.
- Algebra: expressions, equations, inequalities, functions, symbolic manipulation.
- Geometry & Measurement: shapes, congruence, similarity, area, volume, coordinate geometry.
- Probability & Statistics: data representation, measures of central tendency, probability basics, interpreting graphs.
- Pre‑Calculus & Calculus Fundamentals: sequences, limits, derivatives (intro), rates of change.

Learning Objectives
- Students can solve linear equations and explain the steps taken.
- Students can interpret and construct graphs and basic statistics summaries.
- Students can apply geometric reasoning to solve real-world measurement problems.

Suggested Activities
- Problem sets with increasing scaffolding, collaborative problem-solving sessions, math modeling projects, data-collection mini-studies.

Assessment Ideas
- Written exams, open-ended problem tasks, project rubrics, timed arithmetic checks.

Resources
- Interactive graphing tools, math manipulatives, textbooks aligned to grade-level standards, online platforms for practice.

Notes
- Emphasize conceptual understanding and multiple strategies for solving problems. Connect math tasks to science and real-world contexts where possible.

Teaching Guide for Tutor Agent
- Purpose: Provide actionable guidance so the tutor agent can teach math concepts clearly, scaffold understanding, and build problem-solving skills.

Instructional Flow
- Diagnostic: Present a quick problem to identify misconceptions (e.g., fraction vs decimal question).
- Guided Example: Work through a model solution step-by-step, verbalizing reasoning.
- Guided Practice: Provide similar problems with hints and gradually reduce support.
- Independent Application: Assign problem-solving tasks and ask for explanation of steps.

Scaffolding Strategies
- Use concrete-to-abstract progression: manipulatives or visualizations → symbolic representation.
- Chunk multi-step problems into explicit sub-steps and label them.
- Offer hint tiers (gentle hint → worked step → full solution) to keep learners engaged.

Personalization & Signals
- Adapt difficulty using `current_phase` or observed success rate. If the learner makes repeated calculation errors, shift to concept-review mode.

Sample Agent Prompts
- "Solve: 3/4 + 2/5. Explain each step as you go." 
- "I'll show one worked example, then try a similar problem yourself. Ask for a hint if stuck."

Formative Assessment
- Short quizzes, explain-your-reasoning prompts, and error analysis tasks.

Remediation & Extension
- Remediation: focus on number sense exercises and scaffolded practice.
- Extension: offer real-world math modeling projects, proof-style reasoning, or challenge problems.

Teaching Tips
- Encourage learners to explain steps in words, not only calculations. Provide feedback on reasoning clarity as well as correctness.
